Emergency Medical Services

I joined Morganville EMS in high school as a cadet. After completing an EMT-Basic course and earning my EMT certification, I started leading calls and managing patient assessment, treatment, and transport decisions. I continue to volunteer with Morganville EMS and also work part-time as an EMT at Quality Medical Transport.

The calls I deal with can be unpredictable, ranging from simple lift assists to traumatic accidents to suspected strokes. Taking responsibility for patients and sharpening my skills on calls has made me a more decisive and composed leader.

My EMS experience connects directly to my work in biomedical engineering. I use many of the devices I study in class to treat patients, for example, pulse oximeters, AEDs, automatic stretchers, and hemostatic dressings. I want to apply what I learn in biomedical engineering to design devices that perform reliably in the field and improve care for both patients and providers.
